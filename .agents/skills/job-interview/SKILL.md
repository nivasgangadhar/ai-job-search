# Interview preparation in Codex

Read the repository-root `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`, then
[Codex compatibility](../../../docs/CODEX_COMPATIBILITY.md) and the canonical
[interview workflow](../../../.claude/commands/interview.md).
Load the relevant frameworks and exact application archive as that workflow
specifies; a role at the same company is not interchangeable with another role.

Preserve stage-specific preparation, recorded feedback, verified research,
honest STAR answers, and optional practice in the current conversation. Save
prep packs in the canonical ignored archive directory and persist newly
confirmed facts to the profile. Keep the existing approval for appending STAR
examples to the framework. Do not infer submission from a `drafted` tracker row.
